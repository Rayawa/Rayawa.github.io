Shiji · Loci

A HarmonyOS daily time journal. Record activities and their start and end times, review days or weeks, and see time by category. Place and visit records remain available.

GitHub repository

Keep a record of your day

Times and activities

Type or paste several lines of times and activities, or edit with the time picker. Add categories and notes; overnight entries, overlapping periods and single time points are supported.

Day and week views

The journal opens on today. Swipe between dates or jump through the calendar, review entries in reverse time order, and set wake-up and bedtime for each day.

Time statistics

A category pie chart shows where time went. Weekly bars compare one activity across days, using the selected date.

Places and visits

Places have their own tab with map and list views. Each place can hold several visits, with separate notes, moods and dates.

Stored on the device

ArkData stores time entries and places locally. Database migrations retain older place and visit records. The four main tabs are Journal, Places, Statistics and Profile.

In development

Time entries, daily routines and the new navigation are implemented. Maps and location still need service configuration and device checks. Huawei account screens exist; live sign-in and cloud sync are unfinished.
