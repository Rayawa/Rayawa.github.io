Physics Competition

Weak Signal Measurement with Optical Lever Amplification

Designed for weak electrical signals that resist direct measurement, this system uses optical lever amplification. Through a multi-stage chain — electrical signal, mechanical deflection, optical displacement, digital output — it combines a PSD sensor, high-precision ADS1115 sampling and embedded data processing to detect and quantify weak signals indirectly.

Arduino Optical Lever Amplification PSD Sensor Embedded
