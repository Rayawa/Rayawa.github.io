VideoTextGen

An OpenCode-orchestrated local video production workspace. It connects creative treatments, scripts, voice, alignment, direction, storyboards, assets, per-scene rendering, composition, QA, revision and delivery while keeping the runtime, versioned source and approved output cleanly separated.

At a glance

VideoTextGen divides local video production into 12 reviewable stages covering script, voice, subtitle alignment, direction, storyboard, assets, scene rendering, composition, QA, revision, and delivery. Structured contracts track inputs and outputs so changes rerun only affected scenes. The 18-scene example shown here passed technical QA and includes output frames and verification data.

  • Locally reproducibleRuntime, source code, and final video are managed separately.
  • Scene-level revisionImpact analysis and hashes preserve unchanged scenes.
  • Gated deliveryAutomated QA and human review determine the deliverable version.

Purpose and use

Purpose

Turn a video idea into a reviewable local production workflow spanning script, voice, subtitles, storyboard, scene rendering, quality checks, and delivery.

How to use it

Define a project contract and concept, generate voice and alignment, plan scenes and assets, then render each scene. Run technical QA and human review before delivery; analyze affected scenes before a revision.

Technology and implementation

OpenCode coordinates stages; Voicebox handles speech, WhisperX alignment, HTML Motion/Playwright rendering, and FFmpeg assembly. JSON Schema and hashes track inputs and outputs.
